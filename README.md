# Miss Up
"Miss up" is 2D a platform game about a middle-aged woman who is climbing upward which was developed within 24 hours during Haven gamejam in Krakow via Godot.
Game's inspiration was Jump King & Doodle Jump.

## Gameplay mechanics
 - Tutorial & Game Menu
 - Charged jump: hold your "Space" key to make a powerful jump. The longer you hold, the higher you jump.
 - Height counter in meters
 - Three levels by the end of which a story of main character becoming more and more clear..
 - Weather & time of the day, some levels are snowy, some you play in the darkness
 - Interaction with items on the map

## Controls
 | Action | Key
 | --- | --- |
 | Move left | A
 | Move right | D
 | Jump | Space
 | Interact | E
